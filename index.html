PickUp Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

PickUp ("the app") is a pickup sports matchmaking app. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. Data We Collect

  • Location: Your device location is used to find nearby players. It is not stored permanently — only while you have an active search.
  • Anonymous ID: A random anonymous identifier is assigned when you first open the app. No name, email, or phone number is required.
  • Nickname: Optional. If you set a nickname in your profile it is stored and shown to matched players in chat.
  • Chat messages: Messages sent in group chats are stored for the duration of the chat (up to 24 hours) then deleted.
  • Device token: A Firebase Cloud Messaging token is stored to deliver push notifications for matches and messages.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Location is used solely to match you with nearby players.
  • Messages are only visible to players in the same matched group.
  • We do not sell any data to third parties.
  • We do not use data for advertising profiling.

3. Data Retention

  • Queue entries are deleted after 2 hours (unless extended) or when matched.
  • Chat messages are deleted after 24 hours.
  • Your anonymous ID persists unless you uninstall the app.

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services which have their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase — database and authentication
  • Firebase — push notifications and crash reporting
  • Mapbox — maps and location search

5. Children

PickUp is intended for users aged 13 and older.
